Bookings & payments

A booking is confirmed once we receive your completed booking form and a deposit payment. The remaining balance is due by the specified date, which will be shared with you in your booking confirmation.

Prices are quoted in the currency stated and may change because of factors beyond our control, such as currency fluctuations, fuel costs or local taxes.

Changes & cancellations

If you need to change or cancel your trip, tell us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to help, subject to availability and any applicable fees.

Cancellation charges depend on the timing of your request and the terms of our suppliers (hotels, airlines, houseboats and transport). Full details are given at the time of booking.

Travel documents

You are responsible for holding a valid passport, visa (if required) and any other documents needed for your destination before you travel. Please check the latest entry requirements for your destination.

We are happy to provide general guidance, and our visa and passport services can help with applications, but the responsibility for having the correct documents stays with you.

Insurance & safety

We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance covering medical expenses, trip cancellation, lost luggage and other unforeseen events.

Please follow local advice and safety guidelines while travelling. Your safety is important to us, and we will always aim to give you up-to-date information on your destination.

Malabar Routes acts as a travel planner and tour agency, working with trusted local suppliers to create memorable experiences. While we carefully select our partners, we are not liable for circumstances beyond our control, such as natural events, political unrest, transport delays, or changes made by third-party providers.
